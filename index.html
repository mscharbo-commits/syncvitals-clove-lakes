What the QHCP-Approved Care Plan Looks Like — Generated for Every Enrolled Patient
The AI generates — the QHCP decides
SyncVitals generates a condition-based 30/60/90 day plan from the patient's live vitals, diagnoses, and medications. The QHCP reviews each suggested intervention, accepts or rejects individually, adds their own clinical instructions, and formally approves the plan. The QHCP can also upload their own care plan protocol — SyncVitals reads it, enriches it with evidence-based additions, and the physician approves the final version.
Patient receives: A plain-language personal care plan — named to them, written for them, listing every step in language they can follow. Nurse and physician names are on it. Every action is a checkbox. The plan is theirs to keep.
What every care plan includes
✓30/60/90 day phased interventions — Stabilization · Optimization · Sustainability
✓Nutrition guidance — DASH diet, sodium limits, food diary, dietitian referral built in
✓Activity progression — step count targets, walking tolerance, seated exercises for limited-mobility patients
✓Medication adherence — daily reminders, non-adherence documentation, escalation triggers
✓Patient education modules — 4 condition-specific lessons in the patient app, completion tracked
✓Appointment schedule — specific days, provider names, purpose, and modality (phone/telehealth/lab)
✓Emergency action plan — exactly when to call the nurse, when to call 911, patient's personal thresholds
✓Lab tracking — GFR, BMP, potassium, lipid panel at Day 80, results feed back into the AI risk model
The nutrition and activity elements are not add-ons — they are core to every care plan. Every HTN patient gets DASH diet guidance, food diary instructions, and a dietitian referral built into their Day 35 appointment. Every CHF patient gets daily weight monitoring with a ≥5 lb crisis trigger. Every Diabetes patient gets glucose targets, carb guidance, and activity progression. These are clinically governed, QHCP-approved, and fully documented.

Where the 49 minutes comes from — and what it means for Clove Lakes specifically
This is not an efficiency estimate. It is the literal time nurses currently spend walking to each patient's room, setting up equipment, performing the manual vital sign reading, and charting the result — then moving to the next patient and repeating.

With SyncVitals, the device reads and transmits automatically. The AI logs the result, flags anything abnormal, and ranks the patient in the triage queue. The nurse never physically generates the data. That time goes back to patient care.
